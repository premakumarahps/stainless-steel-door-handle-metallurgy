## 2. Deep Scientific & Metallurgical Extraction

### 2.1 Selected Material: AISI 304 Austenitic Stainless Steel (18-8)
- **Chemical Composition**:
  * **Chromium (Cr)**: $18.0\% - 20.0\%$ (Forms self-healing passive $\text{Cr}_2\text{O}_3$ layer $>10.5\%$)
  * **Nickel (Ni)**: $8.0\% - 10.5\%$ (Austenite $\gamma$-FCC stabilizer; lowers $M_s$ below room temp)
  * **Carbon (C)**: $\le 0.08\%$ (Kept minimal to suppress chromium carbide precipitation)
  * **Manganese (Mn)**: $\le 2.0\%$ (Deoxidizer and austenite promoter)
  * **Silicon (Si)**: $\le 1.0\%$ (Deoxidizer and fluidity promoter in casting)
  * **Phosphorus (P)**: $\le 0.045\%$
  * **Sulfur (S)**: $\le 0.030\%$
  * **Iron (Fe)**: Balance ($\sim 68 - 72\%$)
- **Mechanical Properties (As-Heat-Treated)**:
  * Tensile Strength ($\sigma_{\text{UTS}}$): $515 - 620\text{ MPa}$ ($75 - 90\text{ ksi}$)
  * Yield Strength ($\sigma_y$): $205\text{ MPa}$ ($30\text{ ksi}$)
  * Elongation at Break ($\epsilon$): $40\%$ (in $50\text{ mm}$, exceptionally ductile)
  * Hardness: $70 - 90\text{ HRB}$ (Rockwell B)
- **Why Non-Hardenable by Conventional Quenching**:
  Austenitic stainless steels have high concentrations of Cr and Ni which suppress the Martensite Start ($M_s$) temperature to between $-30^{\circ}\text{C}$ and $-70^{\circ}\text{C}$. Consequently, the FCC austenite matrix remains stable at ambient temperature, and no martensitic phase transformation occurs upon rapid cooling. Strengthening is achieved exclusively via cold working (strain hardening) or solid solution strengthening.

### 2.2 The Heat Treatment Plan (Designed by Sadun Premakumara, 210494D)
The core objective of the heat treatment plan for the cast 304 door handle is **Solution Treatment (Solution Annealing)** followed by **Rapid Water Quenching**:

1. **Heating Phase**:
   - Heated steadily to **$1000^{\circ}\text{C} - 1150^{\circ}\text{C}$** (specifically **$1020^{\circ}\text{C} - 1080^{\circ}\text{C}$** conforming to the **GB1200 / ASTM A484** standard).
   - Uniform, gradual heating rate prevents quench distortion and thermal shock in the scalloped geometry.
2. **Soaking Phase (Homogenization)**:
   - Held at $1020^{\circ}\text{C} - 1080^{\circ}\text{C}$ for **60 minutes** (1 hour per 25 mm of section thickness).
   - **Thermodynamic Carbide Dissolution**: Carbon solubility in austenite rises exponentially to $0.34\%$ at $1200^{\circ}\text{C}$ (compared to only $0.02\%$ at $600^{\circ}\text{C}$). Soaking completely dissolves brittle chromium carbides ($M_{23}\text{C}_6$) back into solid solution in the austenite matrix, eliminating chromium-depleted zones adjacent to grain boundaries.
3. **Rapid Cooling Phase (Water Quench)**:
   - **Cooling Medium**: **Agitated Water Quenching**.
   - **Prevention of Sensitization**: Rapid transit through the critical sensitization window ($500^{\circ}\text{C} - 850^{\circ}\text{C}$) freezes carbon in supersaturated solid solution, preventing intergranular chromium carbide reprecipitation.
   - **Suppression of Sigma ($\sigma$) Phase**: Avoids the formation of the brittle, non-magnetic Fe-Cr intermetallic $\sigma$ phase that forms during prolonged exposure at $500^{\circ}\text{C} - 900^{\circ}\text{C}$.
4. **Stress Relief & Dislocation Relaxation**:
   - Thermal activation at solution temperature reduces the hot yield strength below internal casting residual stresses. Dislocation glide and climb relieve residual strains, guaranteeing dimensional stability and preventing **Stress Corrosion Cracking (SCC)**.

### 2.3 Manufacturing & Investment (Lost-Wax) Casting Workflow
Due to the high melting point of 304 stainless steel ($1371^{\circ}\text{C} - 1427^{\circ}\text{C}$ / $2500^{\circ}\text{F} - 2600^{\circ}\text{F}$) and the complex scalloped handle geometry, **Investment Casting** was selected over Sand Casting, Gravity Die Casting, Pressure Die Casting, and Centrifugal Casting:

1. **Pattern Creation**: 3D CAD modeling in SolidWorks + precision wax pattern production via Stereolithography (SL) 3D printing.
2. **Tree Assembly**: 4 wax handle patterns mounted symmetrically onto a central wax pouring sprue to form a 4-cluster **"Wax Tree"** (Report Figure 23).
3. **Ceramic Shell Building**: Dipped into ceramic slurry (zircon/colloidal silica binder) followed by $6 - 8$ coatings of refractory stucco (Portland cement, $\text{CaO}$, silica sand) with $24 - 36$ hours drying.
4. **Dewaxing**: Autoclave steam or flash-fire heating at $160^{\circ}\text{F} - 200^{\circ}\text{F}$ ($71^{\circ}\text{C} - 93^{\circ}\text{C}$) to evacuate molten wax.
5. **Mold Preheating**: Ceramic shell fired to $1800^{\circ}\text{F} - 2200^{\circ}\text{F}$ ($982^{\circ}\text{C} - 1204^{\circ}\text{C}$) to eliminate residual volatiles and prevent metal freezing upon contact.
6. **Pouring & Solidification**: Induction-melted 304 stainless steel poured at $2500^{\circ}\text{F} - 2600^{\circ}\text{F}$ ($1371^{\circ}\text{C} - 1427^{\circ}\text{C}$).
7. **Shell Breakout**: Vibratory knockout table fractures the brittle ceramic mold.
8. **Cutoff & Surface Finishing**: Mechanical abrasive saw cuts handles from the central sprue; linishing and pre-polishing prepare the part for **Solution Heat Treatment**.

### 2.4 Ergonomics & Industrial CAD Specifications
- **Type**: Solid static architectural pull handle (no internal mechanical latch springs or levers to fail from wear).
- **Application**: Residential & commercial main entrance doors (standard $36'' \times 80'' \times 1\text{-}3/4''$ front wooden door).
- **Ergonomic Features**:
  * Scalloped / corrugated wave profile with $2.0\text{ cm}$ pitch along the grip body to match human finger contours.
  * Flared comfort stops at top and bottom to prevent hand slippage.
  * Solid stainless steel construction: Eliminates internal reinforcing ribs because the post-heat-treated 304 alloy possesses superior yield ($205\text{ MPa}$) and tensile strength ($515 - 620\text{ MPa}$).
- **CAD Orthographic Dimensions (SolidWorks Drawing, Report Figure 22)**:
  * Total Handle Length: $24.0\text{ cm} - 25.0\text{ cm}$
  * Center-to-Center Mounting Standoff Span: $16.0\text{ cm}$
  * Mounting Standoff Diameter: $2.0\text{ cm}$
  * Standoff Wall Clearance: $4.0\text{ cm}$
  * Escutcheon Mounting Flange Diameter: $4.0\text{ cm}$
  * Scallop Crest Diameter: $3.0\text{ cm} - 4.0\text{ cm}$
  * Scallop Trough Diameter: $2.0\text{ cm}$
